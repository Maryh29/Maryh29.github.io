03 / Master’s thesis

Does AI support improve
customer replies?

Evaluating speed, reply quality, usability and willingness to adopt a human-reviewed, low/no-code AI workflow.

Context
Master’s research
Role
Design · Build · Evaluate
Tools
n8n · LLM · Mixed methods

The strongest finding

Higher-rated replies.
Greater willingness to use.

AI-supported replies met the quality criterion and received higher blind ratings in this study.

Perceived usability76.43/100

Mean System Usability Scale score

Willingness to use3.95 to 4.81

Mean pre-/post-use rating · out of 5

Mean reply time2.40 to 1.64

Minutes · descriptive reduction

My contribution

Beyond building the workflow.

I designed the prototype and crossover experiment, collected task and interview data, and evaluated performance alongside user adoption perceptions.

What the study shows

Quality and adoption findings were clearer than the speed finding. The time difference was not statistically established (exact p = .297); willingness to use increased after hands-on experience (p = .016).

Exploratory mixed-methods study · n = 7.

The experience participants tested.

Workflow and study detail

Both conditions supplied the same return-policy reference. Quality was evaluated for accuracy, clarity, tone and actionability. Interviews explored usefulness, control and adoption concerns.
